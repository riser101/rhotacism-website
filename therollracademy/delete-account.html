Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your The Rollr Academy account (app by TopSpeech Health LLC) and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email team@topspeech.health from the email address you used to sign in to the app.
  2. Put “Delete my account” in the subject line.
  3. We verify that the request comes from the account holder, then delete the account.

Email us to delete your account

We action requests within 30 days, and normally within a few business days. You will get an email confirming the deletion is complete.

What is deleted

DataOutcome
Account and sign-in identity (name, email address)Deleted
Voice recordings — baseline, weekly progress and practice audioDeleted
Speech assessment results and progress reportsDeleted
Onboarding survey answers, including phone number if providedDeleted
Practice history, streaks and app preferencesDeleted
Push notification tokenDeleted

What is kept, and for how long

DataRetention
Purchase and subscription records, where we must keep them for tax, accounting and fraud-prevention obligations Up to 7 years, as required by law
Aggregated, anonymised usage statistics that can no longer be linked to you or to any individual Retained indefinitely
Backups containing deleted records Purged on the normal backup cycle, within 90 days

Deleting some data without deleting your account

If you want to remove your recordings but keep your account, email the same address and tell us what you would like removed. You can also delete an individual progress recording from inside the app.

Cancelling your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions before requesting deletion.

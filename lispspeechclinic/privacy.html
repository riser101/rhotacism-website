Privacy Policy

TopSpeech lisp program app and Lisp Speech Clinic website

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how TopSpeech Health LLC (“TopSpeech”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you use the TopSpeech lisp program app for iPhone and iPad (the “App”) and the Lisp Speech Clinic website at topspeech.health/lispspeechclinic (the “Website”). TopSpeech Health LLC provides both the App and the Website.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name and email address when you sign in with Apple, Google or an email and password. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we receive Apple’s relay address.
  • Onboarding answers: who the program is for (yourself, your child, or a client), the sounds you want to work on, your voice type, age group and goals.
  • Voice recordings: audio you record during the baseline assessment, reassessments, practice drills and live conversation practice.
  • Face video (optional): if you allow camera access during the baseline assessment, short front-camera clips recorded while you say the test words, used to check tongue and lip placement. You can turn the camera off and complete the assessment with audio only.
  • Assessment results and progress: your lisp type and severity, reports, completed drills, practice days, streaks and app settings.
  • Purchase status: whether you have an active subscription. App Store payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your card details.
  • Usage data: events such as screens viewed, features used, sign-in and purchase events, together with your device model, app version and time zone.
  • Website data: pages visited and cookies set by our analytics tools, and any details you enter in Website forms.

How We Use Your Information

  • To run your assessment, analyze your /s/ and /z/ sounds and build your report
  • To deliver your daily program, compare reassessments with your baseline and sync your progress across devices
  • To check your subscription and give you access to paid content
  • To send service emails, such as password resets, and to answer support requests
  • To understand how the App is used, fix problems and improve the program
  • To improve our speech-analysis models, as described below

How Your Audio and Video Are Processed

  • Recordings are sent over encrypted connections to our secure servers, where your speech is analyzed. Results are returned to the App.
  • During live conversation practice, your audio is processed in real time to give you feedback.
  • Recordings and face video are stored in your account so the App can play them back and compare later recordings with your baseline.
  • We may securely store and use recordings and assessment results to train and improve our speech-analysis models. We will never sell your audio or video, and we do not use them to identify you personally.

Service Providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the App and the Website, and only for that purpose:

  • Cloud hosting, storage and processing providers
  • Speech and language processing providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Sign-in providers and the App Store, for accounts and subscriptions
  • Communication and scheduling providers, only if you contact us or book a call

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal information, recordings or results
  • We do not use your information for advertising
  • The App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies

Data Retention

  • We keep your account data, recordings and results while your account is active, so the App can show your progress over time.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account, recordings, results, onboarding answers and practice history within 30 days.
  • Purchase records may be kept for up to 7 years where the law requires it for tax and accounting.
  • Backups containing deleted records are purged on the normal backup cycle, within 90 days.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Microphone and camera: you can turn access on or off at any time in your device’s Settings.
  • Access, correction and export: email us to get a copy of your data or to correct it.
  • Account deletion: email team@topspeech.health from the email address you use to sign in, with “Delete my account” in the subject line. We verify the request and confirm by email when deletion is complete.
  • Website cookies: you can block or clear cookies in your browser settings.

Children

The App is designed for teens and adults. When a parent uses the App to help a child, the parent should create and manage the account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without a parent’s consent. If you believe a child has given us information without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and Storage

Information is encrypted in transit and encrypted at rest on secure servers in the United States. Access to your recordings and progress is limited to your signed-in account and to the TopSpeech team for support and quality purposes. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If changes are significant, we will notify you in the App or by email.
